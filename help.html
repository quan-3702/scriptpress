How to use ScriptPress

Writing

ScriptPress formats your screenplay as you type, in the industry-standard layout (Courier 12 pt, about one page per minute of screen time).

EnterNext element: after a scene heading comes action, after a character name comes dialogue, after dialogue the next character.
TabChange the element: in action Tab makes a character; in a character name Tab adds a parenthetical; in dialogue Tab makes a parenthetical.
Shift+TabChange the element back.
Ctrl+1…8Scene heading, Action, Character, Parenthetical, Dialogue, Transition, Shot, General.
Shift+EnterNew line inside the same element.
Ctrl+DDual dialogue: on the second character's name, prints the two speeches side by side.
Ctrl+MA note on the current line (not printed).
Ctrl+FFind & replace.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo.
F11Focus mode.

Type INT. or EXT. at the start of a line and it becomes a scene heading. Character names, locations, times of day (DAY, NIGHT…) and transitions are suggested as you type – press Tab or Enter to take a suggestion. In dialogue, type ( on an empty line to start a parenthetical.

Scenes and index cards

The left panel lists every scene with its page; click to jump, drag to move a whole scene. 🗂 Cards shows each scene as an index card: write a short summary, give it a color and drag the cards to restructure your story.

Saving and files

Scripts save automatically on your computer. ⬇ Export makes a PDF, Fountain, Final Draft (.fdx, Pro), Word (.docx, Pro) or plain text file. 📂 Open reads Fountain, text and Final Draft files. Use ☰ → Back up all scripts now and then.

For scripts in languages the PDF font cannot draw (for example Chinese or Japanese), use 🖨 Print → Save as PDF.

Works on Windows and Mac

ScriptPress runs offline in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome on Windows and macOS. Files you export open in Final Draft, Fade In, Highland, WriterDuet and other screenwriting apps.
