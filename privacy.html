ScriptPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. ScriptPress is made by NTQ37.

Your scripts stay on your computer

Everything you write – scripts, title pages, notes, index cards, drafts and voice choices – is stored only in this app on your device. Nothing is uploaded to NTQ37 or anyone else. Files you open or export are read and written only on your computer.

Table read

Table read uses the speech voices installed on your computer (Windows or macOS). Your script is not sent anywhere by ScriptPress; some browsers may offer online voices from the browser maker – choose a local voice if you prefer.

Purchases

ScriptPress Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. NTQ37 does not receive your payment details; the app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
